Privacy Policy – Goblin Workshop

Effective date: October 27, 2026  |  Last updated: October 27, 2026

SinSoft Studios develops Goblin Workshop, an idle clicker game. For privacy questions, email sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.

Current and planned features: The current development version saves progress locally and has no advertising, billing, accounts, cloud saves, analytics, or crash-reporting services. We plan to add AdMob advertising and may offer in-game purchases. The sections below about those features apply only when they are enabled in a future version.

Game Progress and Local Storage

Progress, including coins, upgrades, workers, buildings, achievements, tutorial state, and save times, is stored on your device with a local recovery copy. Save times support progress while you are away. We do not receive your save files or provide cloud synchronization in the current game.

Local progress remains until reset or removed. New game resets progress; to remove all saved data, delete the game's local data, including recovery copies. Device backups are managed separately. We cannot restore or delete a server copy because we do not hold one.

Planned Advertising

We plan to use Google AdMob, initially for optional rewarded ads. An offer will explain its reward before you choose to watch. Core play will remain available without watching ads.

In ad-supported versions, Google and participating advertising providers may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalized ads where permitted and consented to.

Advertising may use identifiers and local storage; sites opened from ads may use cookies. We will not send your save files or support emails as ad targeting data. We may receive ad performance and revenue reports. Google receives SDK data directly. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Other ad providers and linked sites have their own policies.

Advertising Choices

Ad-supported versions will obtain consent where required, including in the EEA (including Norway), the UK, and Switzerland. Using the game or accepting a reward is not consent to personalized advertising. Personalization will be used only where allowed, with required consent and age protections.

Where required, a Privacy choices control will let you review, refuse, or withdraw choices and review participating providers. Refusing personalization will not disable core play. Depending on your choices, ads may be non-personalized, limited, or unavailable; non-personalized ads can still process data for reporting and fraud prevention. Android also provides advertising-ID controls.

Where applicable law provides an opt-out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, use Privacy choices or contact us. Advertising disclosures can fall within these definitions without a direct payment for data.

Planned In-Game Purchases

Purchases are not currently available. Before enabling them, we will update this policy to identify the store and any purchase-verification services, the transaction and ownership data processed, and retention and deletion arrangements. We will explain purchase restoration and how resetting progress affects paid content. No payment-card information is collected by the current game.

Support and Your Rights

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use this to answer requests and keep correspondence only as long as needed for support, follow-up, or legal obligations. Our email provider delivers and stores messages. Please send only information needed for your request.

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on legitimate interests in answering support requests and protecting our services, legal obligations where applicable, and consent for advertising processing and device storage where required. Consent requirements take priority.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of information we hold, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Children's Privacy

The current game does not send player data to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Before introducing advertising or purchases, we will finalize the intended audience and applicable age protections and update this policy. The current game has no age-verification system.

Providers, Security, and Changes

Local saves rely on your device's security. We use reasonable safeguards for support information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google documents encrypted transmission for Mobile Ads SDK data. Service providers may process information outside your country; Google explains its transfer safeguards.

Advertising providers control retention under their own policies. See Google's retention information. Uninstalling or withdrawing consent does not erase information they already hold. Use their privacy request options or contact us for help. Withdrawal affects future processing.

We will update this policy as features and data practices change and revise the date above. Contact: sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.
